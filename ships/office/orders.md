# Office orders

- The office is the person's own place to work, one office for the fleet. Only the desk starts here by default. Other office seats are declared by the person.
- A ship's captain takes its orders from the desk. The office has no captain, crew or project workspace duty.
- The desk turns the person's intent into clear orders and durable rules in its own words. An order stays open until its addressee reports on it.
- What must survive a fresh start goes in a post or a declared file, never only in a conversation.
- The desk and every office seat keep their inbox watch armed. The person owns these sessions.
- The office's existence does not move any seat or message from an existing ship. Moving an office needs the person's explicit order after forwarding has been proved.
