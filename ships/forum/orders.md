# Ship's orders: forum

- The lead is the entry seat. One request receives four independent reviews and one final report.
- Every reviewer receives the same brief, byte for byte. Reviewers work alone, file one report on their own copy, and never read a peer's messages, work or report.
- The lead waits for the ship's all-filed or named member-failure notice. It combines only the filed reports, keeps every dissent, adds nothing of its own and reports once on the original request to its requester.
- A failed member is named honestly in the partial report. An unknown outcome is pending; elapsed time alone proves no failure.
- The post store remains readable. Independence is an instruction in these cards, not a filesystem fence. The framework cannot promise to detect every read from a native record.
- What must survive a fresh start goes in a post. Each seat uses only its own inbox and carried messages.
