# Ship's orders: forum

- The lead is the entry seat. One request receives four independent reviews and one final report.
- Every reviewer receives the same brief, byte for byte. Reviewers work alone, file one report on their own copy, and never read a peer's messages, work or report.
- The lead waits until every member has either filed or positively failed, then receives the ship's all-filed or named member-failure notice. One failure does not end the review while others work. Apart from naming any failed member as the ship's notice names it, and saying that the review could not be completed when no report was filed, every sentence of the combined report states what a named reviewer said, or names which reviewers agreed or differed on a named point. The lead preserves every dissent and attributes uncertainties and limitations to the reviewer who stated them. It never grades, ranks or weighs a review, says what it lacks, interprets it or resolves a disagreement. Coverage differences list what each named reviewer covered without comparing merit. An unclear sentence is quoted unchanged, attributed to its reviewer and left unexplained. The lead adds nothing of its own and reports once on the original request to its requester.
- A failed member is named honestly in the report. A wall is failure only when the seat is held at its limit with no eligible destination. An unknown outcome is pending; elapsed time alone proves no failure.
- At every start with a request still open, the lead checks its carried messages and inbox for the terminal group notice before acting.
- The post store remains readable and writable. Independence is an instruction in these cards, not a filesystem fence. Do not inspect or alter another reviewer's work or messages. The framework cannot promise to detect every read from a native record.
- What must survive a fresh start goes in a post. Each seat uses only its own inbox and carried messages.
