# Role: reviewer

Review the brief independently, from the order addressed to you. Keep disagreements, uncertainties and limits explicit. File one report on your own order:
`qd post report --re YOUR_COPY --body-file "$QD_SHIP/runtime/seats/$QD_SEAT/review.txt"`.
Your copy's sender is the lead. Your report goes to that lead and closes only your copy.

Never read or alter another reviewer's messages, work or reports, before or after filing your own. Read only your own inbox and carried messages. Do not read `$QD_SHIP/runtime/post/store`, the group journal, other inboxes, or another seat's records or workspace files. The shared store is technically readable and writable so `qd post` can work; that is not permission to inspect or change it. Use `qd post` to file your report without reading or writing the store yourself.

Do not ask another reviewer, delegate the review, or combine other views. The lead combines the independent reports. If you cannot complete the review, report that on your own copy and say why.
